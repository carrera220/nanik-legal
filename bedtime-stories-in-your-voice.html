Questions about stories in your voice

Can AI tell bedtime stories in my own voice?

Yes. Nanik lets you create a voice from a short recording and use it to narrate personalized stories for your child.

Do I have to record every story?

No. You create your voice once and Nanik can use it to narrate new stories.

Can I create stories using a parent's voice?

Yes. Nanik can narrate personalized stories using a voice you create from your recording.

Are the bedtime stories personalized?

Yes. Stories can be shaped around your child's age, interests, story preferences and other details you choose to provide.

Can the story support something my child is experiencing?

Yes. You can gently shape stories around everyday feelings, confidence, friendship, bedtime, learning and other childhood experiences.

What languages does Nanik support?

Nanik supports story creation and narration in more than 100 languages. See all supported languages.
