Custom tales in your voice

Record about 10 seconds once. Nanik narrates new adventures so your child hears a familiar voice, even when you’re busy or away. Prefer not to record? Choose a storyteller style inspired by classic children's authors like Hans Christian Andersen, the Brothers Grimm, Astrid Lindgren, or Roald Dahl.

Watch their toys come alive

Take a picture of their favorite toy or object. Nanik turns it into a magical illustrated character that stars in the story.

Build any world you imagine

Pick Forest, Cave, Village, city, Mars, underwater, or type your own place. Set the perfect scene for their adventure.

Gently support their day
