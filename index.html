True-voice bedtime stories in 100+ languages

Nanik is a bedtime-story app for parents. Describe a hero, or snap a photo of a beloved toy, and Nanik writes a warm story, illustrates it, and reads it aloud in your true voice, or in the style of a classic children's storyteller.

Custom tales in your voice

Record about 10 seconds once. Nanik narrates new adventures so your child hears a familiar voice, even when you’re busy or away. Prefer not to record? Choose a storyteller style inspired by classic children's authors like Hans Christian Andersen, the Brothers Grimm, Astrid Lindgren, or Roald Dahl.

Watch their toys come alive

Take a picture of their favorite toy or object. Nanik turns it into a magical illustrated character that stars in the story.

Build any world you imagine

Pick Forest, Cave, Village, city, Mars, underwater, or type your own place. Set the perfect scene for their adventure.

Gently support their day

Weave today’s feelings into the story: calm, courage, healthy habits, or whatever your child needs tonight.

A library of your own

Every story stays in My Library, with hero, world, and what the story supports. Replay anytime, or tell it with voice.

Turn stories into a series

Love the script? Keep the same hero and generate the next chapter: new world, new description, same beloved character.

Ready for bedtime?

Free: 2 stories to try. Nanik Plus: 30 stories a month, up to 3 voice clones, 100+ languages, and a 3-day free trial on yearly.
