Bedtime stories in 100+ languages

Nanik writes story text and narrates it aloud in more than 100 languages, so a bedtime story feels natural in whatever language your family speaks at home.

Armenian, English, and Russian are featured first in the app, since they are Nanik's primary story languages. Beyond that, narration covers 102 languages in total, from major European languages to many regional and world languages.

Frequently asked questions

Do you support my language?

Almost certainly. Nanik covers 102 narration languages. If you do not see your language above, contact us at info@nanik.app and we will confirm.

Can I mix languages, like an English story with Armenian narration?

Story language and narration language are chosen separately in the app, so you can generate a story and choose a narration language that fits your family.

Does my cloned voice work in every language?

Voice cloning quality is best in widely-supported languages. See True Voice for details on how voice cloning works.
