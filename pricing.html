What counts as a story?

Each personalized story you create counts toward your plan's story limit. Listening to narration (voiceover) is not separately metered. Stories are designed for about a 3-minute read-aloud; age bands change vocabulary and complexity, not the overall length.

Frequently asked questions

What is included for free vs Nanik Plus?

Free includes 3 personalized stories lifetime, 1 voice clone, and 1 child profile. Plus Monthly: 60 stories/month at $9.99, unlimited voice clones, and unlimited child profiles. Plus Yearly: the same Plus features at $59.99 (Save 50% vs $119.88 if billed monthly) and a 7-day free trial.

Is there a free trial?

Yes. Nanik Plus Yearly includes a 7-day free trial. You are not charged until the trial ends, and you can cancel anytime before then.

How do I cancel?

For web subscriptions, open your Nanik dashboard billing portal (or the link in your Dodo receipt email) to cancel. For App Store purchases, manage subscriptions in iOS Settings or the App Store profile.

Does upgrading unlock more languages?

No, all 100+ languages are available on both Free and Nanik Plus. Nanik Plus increases your story and voice-clone limits and unlocks advanced storytelling.
