What counts as a story?

Each personalized story you create counts toward your plan's story limit. Listening to narration (voiceover) is not separately metered. Stories are designed for about a 3-minute read-aloud; age bands change vocabulary and complexity, not the overall length.

Frequently asked questions

What is included for free vs Nanik Plus?

Free includes 2 personalized stories lifetime and 1 voice clone. Nanik Plus unlocks 30 stories per month, up to 3 voice clones, and advanced storytelling. Live prices appear on the in-app paywall.

Is there a free trial?

Yearly Nanik Plus may include a free trial when offered by Apple (currently 3 days). Trial length and the price after the trial are shown before purchase.

How do I cancel?

Manage or cancel your subscription in Settings > your name > Subscriptions on iOS, or App Store > profile > Subscriptions, and select Nanik. Cancel at least 24 hours before the period ends to avoid renewal.

Does upgrading unlock more languages?

No, all 100+ languages are available on both Free and Nanik Plus. Nanik Plus increases your story and voice-clone limits and unlocks advanced storytelling.
