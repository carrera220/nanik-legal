Privacy Policy

Effective date: April 30, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Nanik ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, discloses, and protects personal information when you use the Nanik mobile application and related services (the "Service").

1. Who We Are

Nanik is a storytelling app that helps parents generate personalized stories and narration experiences for children.

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at: gor.kroyan@gmail.com

2. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly, information generated through use of the Service, and limited technical data.

2.1 Information You Provide

  • Account and sign-in information (for example, Apple Sign-In data and account identifiers)
  • Child profile data you enter (such as name, age, and interests)
  • Story prompts and context you provide
  • Photos you choose to submit as story hero references (for example, a photo of a toy)
  • Subscription and purchase selections
  • Support messages or feedback you send us

2.2 Content and Usage Data

  • Story requests and generated story output
  • Voice-related content you submit when using voice features (for example, voice samples and text-to-speech input text)
  • Usage metrics related to feature consumption (for example, reading and voiceover minutes used, remaining quotas, and billing cycle usage)
  • Entitlement/subscription status and purchase event metadata

2.3 Technical Information

  • Device/app metadata needed to operate the Service
  • Authentication/session tokens and user IDs
  • Error and operational logs necessary for reliability and abuse prevention

3. How We Use Information

We use information to:

  • Provide and maintain the Service
  • Generate personalized stories and narration
  • Authenticate users and secure accounts
  • Manage subscriptions, entitlements, and usage limits
  • Process and validate in-app purchases
  • Improve app quality, performance, and safety
  • Respond to support inquiries and enforce our terms

4. Legal Bases (where applicable)

Depending on your location, we process personal information based on:

  • Performance of a contract (providing the Service you request)
  • Legitimate interests (security, fraud prevention, analytics, service improvement)
  • Consent (where required by law)
  • Legal obligations (compliance with applicable law)

5. How We Share Information

We do not sell personal information. We share data only as needed to provide the Service, including with:

  • Cloud infrastructure and database providers
  • AI and speech service providers used to generate story and voice features
  • Subscription/purchase platforms and processors
  • Analytics, security, and error-monitoring providers
  • Legal/regulatory authorities when required by law

Third-party processing is limited to the purposes described in this policy and subject to contractual/privacy safeguards where applicable.

6. Payments and Subscriptions

In-app purchases are processed by Apple App Store and/or Google Play. We do not receive or store full payment card details. We may process subscription event metadata (for example, entitlement state, renewal/cancellation events, and purchase identifiers) to manage access and limits.

7. Children's Privacy

Nanik is intended to be used by parents or guardians. We expect adults to provide child-related information and supervise use. We collect only the information reasonably necessary to provide personalized storytelling features.

If you believe a child provided personal information without appropriate authorization, contact us at gor.kroyan@gmail.com and we will take appropriate steps.

8. Data Retention

We retain data only as long as necessary for the purposes described in this policy, including to:

  • Provide active Service features
  • Resolve disputes and enforce agreements
  • Meet legal, tax, accounting, and security obligations

Retention periods vary by data type and legal requirements.

9. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards to protect personal information. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

10. International Transfers

Your information may be processed in countries other than your own. Where required, we use appropriate safeguards for international data transfers.

11. Your Rights and Choices

Depending on your location, you may have rights to:

  • Access, correct, or delete personal information
  • Restrict or object to certain processing
  • Request data portability
  • Withdraw consent (where processing is consent-based)
  • Appeal decisions or lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority

You can delete your account and associated data at any time from the Account screen in the app. To exercise other rights, contact gor.kroyan@gmail.com.

12. California Privacy Notice (if applicable)

California residents may have rights under applicable law (including CCPA/CPRA), such as the right to know, delete, and correct certain personal information. We do not sell personal information as defined by applicable California law.

13. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will post the updated version in the app and/or on our website with a revised effective date.
