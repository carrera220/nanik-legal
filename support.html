Frequently asked questions

How do I create a story?

Tap the create button in your library, describe the hero and theme (or snap a photo of a favorite toy), pick a voice, and Nanik will write, illustrate, and narrate a personalized bedtime story.

How do I manage or cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple. Open Settings > your name > Subscriptions on your iPhone, or App Store > your profile > Subscriptions, and select Nanik.

How do I restore a purchase?

Open the paywall screen in the app and tap "Restore purchases" while signed in with the same Apple ID you used to subscribe.

How do I delete my account?

Open the Account screen in the app and choose "Delete account". This permanently removes your account and associated data. You can also email us and we'll take care of it.

Is my child's information safe?

Nanik is designed for parents and guardians to operate. See our Privacy Policy for details on what we collect and how it's used.
