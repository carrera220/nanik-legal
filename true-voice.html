True voice, not a generic AI voice

Record about 10 seconds once. Nanik clones your true voice, so your child hears you reading a new story, even on nights you're busy, traveling, or away.

What "true voice" means

Nanik builds a real clone of your own voice from a short recording, then uses it to narrate new bedtime stories. It is not a generic text-to-speech voice, and it is not a celebrity or actor's voice, it's yours.

How it works

  1. Record ~10 seconds. Read a short prompt in the language you'll speak.
  2. Save with consent. Name the voice and confirm you have the right to use it.
  3. Narrate any story. Choose your voice for any story in your library, in any supported language.

Privacy basics

  • Voice samples are used only to build your narration voice for your stories.
  • Free accounts can keep 1 voice clone; Nanik Plus allows up to 3.
  • Only record a voice you have the right to use.
  • See the full Privacy Policy for details on storage and retention.

Frequently asked questions

Is it really my voice, or a generic AI voice?

It's a true clone of your own voice, built from the short sample you record. It is not a generic text-to-speech voice, and it is not a celebrity or actor's voice.

How long does the recording need to be?

About 9–10 seconds, read clearly in the language you'll speak for narration.

Whose voice can I clone?

Only your own voice, or a voice you have explicit permission to use. You confirm this with a consent checkbox when saving a voice.

Can I delete a voice clone?

Yes, you can manage and remove voice clones from your account. See Support for details.
